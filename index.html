Hello, I’m Rachel.

I am a social design practitioner with a lifelong commitment to co-creating solutions for a more equitable, sustainable world.

My work applies systems thinking, human-centred design, participatory methods, and strategic communication, with the goal of building the social and digital infrastructure that communities need to leverage sociopolitical change.
